# Sing Malayalam brief

Goal: Create a Malayalam counterpart to TheSinging.Bible and publish it from `justin4884k/SingMalayalam` on Vercel.
Audience: Malayalam-speaking Christians who want to hear and meditate on scripture.
Approach: One-page, mobile-first hymn player using public-domain 1910 Malayalam scripture, a traditional slow accompaniment, and male/female browser voice modes.
Scope: Four representative passages, chapter library, playback/progress/volume, favorites, sharing, responsive design, and source attribution.
Risk: Malayalam speech voices vary by device.
Mitigation: Use `ml-IN` speech when available and always provide a generated hymn accompaniment plus visible scripture text.
Success: The deployed page is immediately recognizable, playable, navigable, and accessible on desktop and mobile.
