# Sing Malayalam design contract

- Direction: reverent editorial warmth; a quiet Kerala-church hymnbook rather than a streaming dashboard.
- Palette: midnight blue-black `#172228`, parchment `#f3ead7`, liturgical gold `#d2a75f`, restrained rust `#96452f`.
- Typography: Noto Serif Malayalam for scripture and display; Noto Sans Malayalam for controls and labels.
- Layout: split first viewport with a brief invitation and one dominant, usable scripture player.
- Shape: soft paper card, restrained round controls, fine rules, no generic glass panels.
- Motion: only gentle waveform breathing and direct interaction feedback; reduced-motion respected.
- Accessibility: Malayalam document language, semantic controls, visible focus states, AA contrast, touch targets at least 40px.
